# 🧭 First Step Plan – Wheelchair Taxi Website Project (Enhanced)

## 🎯 Objective
The goal is not just to build a website, but to:

> Increase real bookings, reduce wasted ad spend, and build long-term organic traffic.

---

## 🚀 Step 1: Client Discovery (Most Important)

Understand the client’s business and current problems.

### Key Questions to Ask

1. What is your main goal?
   - More bookings?
   - More phone calls?
   - WhatsApp enquiries?
   - Reduce wasted ad spend?

2. How are you currently getting customers?
   - Google Ads?
   - Google Maps?
   - Facebook?

3. What problems are you facing?
   - Fake or irrelevant clicks?
   - Paying for ads without real enquiries?
   - Difficulty converting visitors into bookings?

---

## 💡 Step 2: Define the Real Value

You are NOT just building a website.

You are solving:

> "Reducing wasted ad spend and increasing real customer bookings"

---

## 🧱 Step 3: MVP Website (Conversion-Focused)

The website must act as a **conversion machine**.

### Core Features:
- Clear service description (Wheelchair Taxi)
- 📞 Call Now button (primary CTA)
- 💬 WhatsApp contact button
- 🟢 WeChat contact (if target users require it)
- Simple booking form (backup option)

---

## 📊 Step 4: Conversion Tracking System (Key Value)

Track which ad clicks become real customers.

### Track:
- Call button clicks
- WhatsApp clicks
- Form submissions
- gclid (Google Click ID)

### Purpose:
- Identify valuable traffic
- Identify wasted clicks

---

## 💰 Step 5: Use Conversion Data to Optimise Ads

Analyse data like:

| Keyword | Clicks | Calls |
|--------|--------|------|
| wheelchair taxi | 50 | 10 ✅ |
| free transport | 40 | 0 ❌ |

### Actions:
- Remove low-quality keywords
- Increase budget on high-converting keywords

Result:
- Fewer clicks
- More bookings
- Lower cost per customer

---

## 🛡️ Step 6: Traffic Quality & Protection

Reduce impact of suspicious traffic.

### Techniques:
- IP tracking and basic blocking
- Rate limiting (e.g., Cloudflare)
- Detect repeated abnormal clicks

Note:
- This reduces abuse but does not stop ad charges completely

---

## 🌐 Step 7: SEO (Long-Term Growth Strategy)

Build organic traffic to reduce reliance on ads.

### Focus:
- Local keywords (e.g., wheelchair taxi Christchurch)
- Service pages
- Clear descriptions and pricing

### Benefits:
- Free long-term traffic
- Increased trust

---

## 🤖 Step 8: AI & Search Visibility

Make the website visible in AI-powered search.

### Requirements:
- FAQ sections
- Structured content
- Clear service information
- Schema markup (LocalBusiness)

### Goal:
- Appear in Google AI results and AI tools

---

## 🧠 Step 9: Positioning (Very Important)

Do NOT position as:

> "I build websites"

Position as:

> "I help reduce wasted ad spend, increase bookings, and build long-term customer traffic"

---

## 💬 First Message to Client (Template)

Hi, I’d love to help you build a website that can improve your Google Ads performance and reduce wasted clicks.

Before I start, could I understand a bit more about your current setup?

- How are customers contacting you now (calls, WhatsApp, Facebook)?
- Are you mainly running Google Ads or relying on Google Maps?
- What kind of enquiries do you want more of?

This will help me design something that brings you real bookings instead of just traffic.

---

## ✅ Summary

Your first action is not coding.

👉 It is understanding the client deeply and designing a system that:
- Converts visitors into customers
- Tracks real value
- Optimises advertising
- Builds long-term traffic (SEO + AI visibility)
