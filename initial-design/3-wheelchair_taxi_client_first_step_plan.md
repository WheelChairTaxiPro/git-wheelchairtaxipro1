# 🧭 First Step Plan – Wheelchair Taxi Website Project

## 🎯 Objective
Before building anything, the goal is to clearly understand the client’s business and real problem so the solution delivers value (more bookings, less wasted ad spend).

---

## 🚀 Step 1: Client Discovery (Most Important)

The first thing to do is talk to the client and gather key information.

### Key Questions to Ask

1. **What is your main goal?**
   - More bookings?
   - More phone calls?
   - WhatsApp enquiries?
   - Reduce wasted ad spend?

2. **How are you currently getting customers?**
   - Google Ads?
   - Google Maps?
   - Facebook?

3. **What problems are you facing?**
   - Are you getting fake or irrelevant clicks?
   - Are you paying for ads without real enquiries?
   - Do customers struggle to contact or book you?

---

## 💡 Step 2: Define the Real Value

You are NOT just building a website.

You are solving:

> "Reducing wasted ad spend and increasing real customer bookings"

This positioning is critical for winning the client.

---

## 🧱 Step 3: Define a Simple MVP Website

Before building a full system, start with a simple, effective version.

### MVP Features:
- Clear service description (Wheelchair Taxi)
- Call Now button (high visibility)
- Simple booking form
- Conversion tracking (calls + form submissions)
- Integration with Google Ads tracking (gclid)

---

## 🧠 Step 4: Position Yourself Correctly

Instead of saying:

> "I build websites"

Position yourself as:

> "I help reduce wasted ad spend and bring in real bookings"

This makes your service much more valuable.

---

## 💬 First Message to Client (Template)

Hi, I’d love to help you build a website that can improve your Google Ads performance and reduce wasted clicks.

Before I start, could I understand a bit more about your current setup?

- How are customers contacting you now (calls, WhatsApp, Facebook)?
- Are you mainly running Google Ads or relying on Google Maps?
- What kind of enquiries do you want more of?

This will help me design something that brings you real bookings instead of just traffic 🙂

---

## ✅ Summary

Your FIRST action is not coding.

👉 It is understanding the client deeply so you can solve the right problem.

Once this is done, you can confidently move to:
- Proposal writing
- Website design
- Monetisation strategy
- Google Ads optimisation
