## 💰 Pricing Explanation（收費說明）

### ENGLISH
Our pricing is based on the taxi meter fare plus a service or booking fee depending on the vehicle type.

### 🚕 New Small Wheelchair Taxi (Promotion)
- Capacity: Up to 2–3 passengers
- Fare: Taxi meter + **$100 service fee** (Original price: $120)

### 🚐 Standard (Older) Wheelchair Taxi
- Capacity: Up to 4 passengers
- Fare: Taxi meter + **$120 service fee**

### 🚙 New Large Luxury Wheelchair Taxi
- Capacity: Up to 6 passengers
- Fare: Taxi meter + **$150–$180 booking fee**
- If no specific vehicle is requested and assigned randomly: **$120 booking fee**

⭐ Please clearly state the number of passengers when booking.

Vehicle type (new/old/luxury) will be arranged based on availability at the time of booking, and pricing will be adjusted accordingly.

---

### 🚕 新款細輪椅的士（推廣優惠）
- 最多乘客：2–3 位
- 收費：咪錶 + **$100 服務費**（原價 $120）

## 🔄 Booking Flow（預約流程說明）

### ENGLISH
1. User fills in the booking form in the app or website.
2. User clicks the **Submit Booking** button.
3. The booking information is sent to the backend system.
4. The system will automatically send a booking email to **KK Leung**.
5. KK Leung will review the request and contact the customer via phone, WhatsApp, or email to confirm the booking details (availability, pricing, and driver arrangement).

---

### 中文（繁體）
1. 用戶於網站或應用程式填寫預約表。
2. 點擊「提交預約」按鈕。
3. 預約資料會傳送至後端系統。
4. 系統會自動發送預約電郵至 **梁生（KK）**。
5. 梁生會審核預約，並透過電話、WhatsApp 或電郵聯絡客戶確認預約詳情（包括車輛安排、收費及司機）。
