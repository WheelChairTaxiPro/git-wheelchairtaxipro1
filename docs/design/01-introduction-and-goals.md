## Status

This chapter is a **stub**. Content will be authored in **Phase 2** of the approved plan.

## Planned scope

- **Product overview**: what Wheelchair Taxi Pro is, for whom, and why it exists
- **Target users**: wheelchair-using riders in Hong Kong and their carers; secondary — dispatch staff receiving bookings
- **Top-3 quality goals** (in priority order): (1) SEO dominance on key HK queries + AI answer engines, (2) accessibility for wheelchair and broader-disability users (WCAG 2.2 AA), (3) bilingual UX (zh-HK default, EN mirror) with fast mobile-first performance
- **Stakeholders**: owner/operator, riders, carers, dispatch staff, drivers (Phase 2), Hong Kong local SEO reviewers
- **Phase 1 KPIs** (month-3 targets from README): organic sessions, top-10 keywords, Google Business views, backlinks, reviews
